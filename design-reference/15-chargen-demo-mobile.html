Character generation — mobile

Clickable. The same engine as page 14, in the phone shell: the rail becomes a progress line, the derived panel becomes a tappable bar. Spec: CHARGEN-WIZARD.md.

Two taps reveal what the rail held

Tapping the title opens the full step list — completed steps are tappable, so back-navigation is the same as on desktop. Tapping the bottom bar opens the character sheet: HP, AC, DC, all six abilities and the slot table.

Thumb-first

Every target is at least 44px. Continue is sticky at the bottom and states the blocker on itself — “Choose 2 more” rather than a warning after the fact. The step body is the only thing that scrolls.

Level 1 → 10, on a phone

Use Skip to level 1 complete, then press Level up repeatedly. Each level asks only what it needs: a tradition at 2, an ability improvement at 4 and 8, a new cantrip at 4 and 10, two spellbook spells every level, and a prepare step throughout.

Real data

215 wizard spells, the slot table and the per-level features come from class-wizard.json, spells-phb.json and gendata-spell-source-lookup.json.
