Character generation wizard

Guiding a player from nothing to a playable character — phone first, two rule editions, one engine. Spec: CHARGEN-WIZARD.md.

Two orders, one engine

The 2024 rules moved ability increases and the origin feat onto the background, which inverts the dependency order: scores cannot be finalised before the background is known. The corpus carries both editions, so the wizard branches rather than picking a winner. Edition is never asked — it follows from the world's declared source set.

Desktop — the derived panel teaches the system

Every pick recomputes HP, AC, saves and modifiers before it is committed, and marks what changed. A player who watches AC go 10 → 16 on taking chain mail has learned something the rulebook spent a paragraph on.

Phone — the primary target

Eight labelled steps do not fit at 375px, and must not become a horizontal scroll the player has to discover. The rail collapses to one progress line; tapping the title opens the full step list as a sheet. The derived panel becomes a one-line bar that expands on tap — consequence-while-choosing matters just as much on a phone.

Right: every proficiency names where it came from. Overlap between class, race and background is the single most common confusion in 5e character creation, and naming the source at the point of choice removes it. Blocked options are disabled with the reason on them — never a warning after the fact.

Choices are data, not per-class code

The corpus encodes choices structurally — choose, weighted, defaultData — so one engine walks all of them. A new book must not require new UI code.

choose · pick N of a list {"choose":{"from":[…],"count":2}} Fighter's two skills. Renders as a multi-select that stops at the count.
weighted · assign a distribution {"weighted":{"from":["int","wis","cha"],"weights":[2,1]}} 2024 background ASI. Not a multi-select — it renders as “+2 to ___, +1 to ___”.
defaultData · equipment A/B {"a":["chain mail|phb"],"b":[…]} Radio groups. Key casing differs by edition — match case-insensitively or silently lose options.
equipmentType · deferred picker {"equipmentType":"weaponMartial"} Must expand into a filtered searchable item list, never render as literal text.

Honesty rules

Block the impossible, don't warn afterwards

Typed prerequisites ({"ability":[{"str":13}]}) disable the option with the reason on it. prerequisite.other is free prose and cannot be machine-checked — it becomes a confirmation checkbox the player ticks, never a silent allow or a silent block.

Going back never loses work silently

Changing class after picking skills marks the affected steps “needs review” in the rail and says what became invalid and why. Silent data loss on back-navigation is the worst failure this flow can have.

A missing book is not a dead end

An option referencing a book outside the world's source set renders in the “not in your books” state, and its tooltip offers to add the book. The wizard never pretends the option does not exist.

A draft is a real, saved thing

State persists from the first step. The choice stack keeps chosen options, not resolved values, so a corpus update or a source-set change re-derives the character instead of corrupting it.
