Playing the character

Clickable. Lyra Mooncaller, a level 10 evocation wizard — the build from page 14, now at the table. Spend slots, take damage, roll saves, cast spells, rest. Spell mechanics come from the real PHB data.

Desktop

Three columns: who you are on the left (and it never moves), what you do in the middle, what you spend on the right. Hover any spell, ability or condition for a tooltip.

Try it

Cast Fireball — a level 3 slot disappears. Cast Haste — a concentration badge appears; take damage and a concentration check is logged. Tap a spell slot pip to spend or recover it. Long rest restores everything.

Mobile

The same state, same handlers. HP controls sit above the fold on the spells tab, because that is what a player touches most; the bottom bar carries the five views. Tooltips open as taps, never hover.

Everything a player touches mid-turn

HP ±1/±5 with temp-HP absorbed first · spell slot pips · cast buttons that actually spend the slot · attack and damage rolls · ability checks, saves and skills · the advantage three-state · conditions · short and long rest · a dice log.

Advantage is three states, never a checkbox

Dis / Normal / Adv, because the rule has three states. It rolls two d20s and shows both in the log, so the player can see which was taken.

Tooltips follow the input, not the screen width

matchMedia('(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)') decides. A mouse hovers after 350 ms; a finger taps. Esc closes. An iPad in landscape is 1024px wide with no hover — width would get it wrong.

Real spell data

Casting time, range, components, duration, concentration, save type and scaling dice all come from spells-phb.json. Fire Bolt shows 2d10 because the character is level 10 — the next step up is 3d10 at level 11. Read from scalingLevelDice, not typed in.
