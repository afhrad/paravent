# Playing with Paravent

Your character lives on your phone: every roll, every hit point, every charge of your
wand. The gamemaster sees it all as it happens, so you never have to call out a number
twice. This page walks through what you will use at the table.

---

## 1. Your character

The home page lists your characters and, while one is running, the fight. **New
character** in the user menu starts the wizard: species, class, abilities, background,
skills, equipment and details, one step at a time, with what each choice gives shown as
you go. You can leave it and come back where you were.

The sheet has four views on a phone, in the bar at the bottom:

- **Play** — hit points, attacks and spells: what you use on your turn.
- **Stats** — abilities, saving throws, skills, and your armour class, speed and the rest.
- **Track** — spell slots, resources, conditions, exhaustion, rests, and any disease,
  poison or curse you carry.
- **Gear** — coins, what you carry and how heavy it is, your lifestyle, magic items.

The **log** button in the top bar opens your dice log.

## 2. Rolling

Tap a number to roll it: an ability, a save, a skill, an attack, damage. The total lands
in the log, the gamemaster's included, with every die and bonus.

- **Adv** / **Dis** beside the rolls give the next roll advantage or disadvantage. Your
  conditions set it themselves where the rules say so (a poisoned character rolls checks
  with disadvantage), and the sheet tells you why.
- **Private** keeps a roll between you and the gamemaster.
- A natural 20 or a natural 1 lights up, and if dice sounds are on (user menu), you hear
  them.

## 3. Hit points and the day's wear

The hit point box takes damage, healing and temporary hit points: type the amount and
press **Damage**, **Heal** or **Temp**, or use the quick buttons. Temporary hit points go
first; concentration asks for its save when you take damage; at 0 you roll death saves on
the sheet.

On **Track**: tick a condition on or off, raise or lower exhaustion, spend a hit die, and
take a **Short rest** or **Long rest**. A long rest brings back hit points, slots and
resources (unless the gamemaster's road or a disease says otherwise).

## 4. Spells

Each spell has its damage dice, **Call for saves** while you are in a fight, and **Cast**.
Pick a higher slot level where you have one to cast it higher; the slot is spent when you
cast. A healing spell asks whom to heal. **Prepare spells** after a long rest, if your
class prepares them. A concentration spell shows as held until you drop it or lose it.

## 5. Gear and magic items

- **Equip** armour, shields and weapons: they set your armour class and your attacks.
- **Attune** to a magic item that asks for it, up to three. Some items are only for some
  characters (a cleric's staff); the sheet says so if yours is not one of them.
- A magic item with **charges** shows them as dots: **−** spends one, **+** gives one
  back. They come back by themselves when the item says so, usually at dawn by the
  world's clock.
- The spells an item casts are on the spells list under **From items**, cast like your
  own, the item's charges spent instead of a slot. A wand that allows it can spend more
  charges for a higher level.
- Potions have **Drink**, torches and lanterns **Light**, and scrolls their spell.
- When the gamemaster opens a shop, it appears on your sheet: buy, and sell what you no
  longer need.

## 6. In a fight

When a fight starts, a strip appears above your sheet with the round and whose turn it
is. Switch between your character and **Fight** with the two buttons at the top.

- **Initiative**: roll it on your sheet when the gamemaster asks; it goes straight into
  the order.
- On your turn, **Your turn!** shows in big letters.
- Tap someone in the order to make them your **Target**, so the gamemaster's hit lands
  where you aimed, or to see their **Details**: what your character could tell by
  looking, what the party has seen it do, and what the gamemaster has told you.
- When a monster's spell or breath asks for your save, it appears on your phone: roll it
  there. The gamemaster never shows you the DC.

## 7. When the gamemaster asks

A green box on your sheet — *The gamemaster asks for Perception* — is a check called for
you. Roll it from the box. A trap's or a poison's save can come the same way. You only
see your own total; the gamemaster decides what it means.

## 8. Between sessions

- **Level up** appears when the gamemaster grants a level: you answer only what the new
  level brings.
- **Downtime** spends days the gamemaster gives the party: crafting, training, research,
  carousing and more.
- **Followers**, **Bastion** (from level 5) and **Story** sit in the sheet's head.
- The **Journal** holds the letters, notes and pictures the gamemaster gave you, the
  session notes, and **Party notes** the whole group writes together.

## 9. Safety

The **X-card** on your sheet and in the fight tells the gamemaster — and only them — to
change course, without a word and without your name in the log. Under **Safety tools**
you can suggest a line or a veil; it reaches the gamemaster without your name.
