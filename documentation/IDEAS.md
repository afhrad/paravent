# Unfinished ideas

What is not built yet but was found worth doing, with where it came from. Nothing here is
decided: an idea becomes work when the user picks it, and moves to PROGRESS.md when built.
Sizes are rough — **S** a day's change, **M** a few, **L** a feature of its own.

## From the Dungeon Master's Guides (surveyed 2026-11-16)

Both guides were read chapter by chapter against what Paravent does. Already covered and so
not listed: treasure and encounter tables, encounter shapes and ratings, NPCs, the creature
builder, chases, traps, travel (weather, climate, altitude, food and water), factions and
renown, quests, followers, bastions, downtime, the calendar and clock, session notes,
milestone levelling, the reference screens, safety tools.

### Smaller

- **Supernatural gifts** — grant a blessing or charm to a character, a charm's uses kept as a
  resource (gifts can be browsed; epic boons arrive as feats). S
- **Divine intervention** — a pantheon per world and the intervention roll. S
- **Marks of prestige** — titles, land, medals and favours on a character's story. S
- **Running costs** — businesses and garrisons charged by the clock, like lifestyle and wages. S
- **Follower loyalty** — a score that events move. S
- **Attitude** — an NPC's or contact's attitude toward the party, with when they were last seen. S
- **Quick experience** — an ad-hoc XP award for a non-combat challenge, with a switch for
  absent characters (quests and fights already award). S
- **Objects and doors** — AC, hit points and break DC by material and size; an object as a
  target in the tracker. S
- **Siege equipment** in the tracker, with its crew. S
- **Hazards by level** — environmental damage and DCs scaled to the party, as traps are. S
- **Random checks in a dungeon** over game time, not only on the road. S
- **Treasure by a monster's preferences**, and trade goods. S
- **Scaling lethality** — gentler or harsher death saves as a world switch. S
- **Session zero** — the expectations sheet kept per world, beside lines and veils. S

### Bigger

- **Planar effects** — the rules of the plane the party is on, rolled or applied. M
- **Villain and adventure tables** — scheme, methods and weakness for the NPC generator;
  premises, hooks and twists as seeds, also for Claude's `new_adventure`. S–M
- **Character variants** — hero points, proficiency dice, honor and sanity, rest variants
  such as gritty realism, as world switches. M
- **Sentient items and artifacts** — a sentient item's mind and purpose, an artifact's
  properties rolled. M
- **Random dungeon generator** (2014) — passages, doors, chambers and their contents into a
  chapter's skeleton. L
- **Spell, magic item and background builders**, published as homebrew like the creature
  builder's creatures. L

## From reviews and passes of the app

- **A player's guide in the app** — the gamemaster has *Between the fights*; players have
  nothing on downtime, bastions, followers, the shop and scrolls from their side. A short
  help page linked from the sheet. S (proposed 2026-11-16)
- **The shop on a phone** — every shelf item has its own count and Buy, so the page runs
  very long; the search and category chips narrow it. A compact row, or the list shown only
  once a category or search is chosen. S (phone pass 2026-11-16)
- **One shared form for the sheet's buttons** — about 85 small forms become one; measured
  to save about 5 of the sheet's 38 ms, and it touches every button, so it waits. M
  (measured 2026-11-16)
- **Left to the players, on purpose** (review 2026-11-16) — training days typed by the
  player in a world without either rulebook's training rules, and ending a Polymorph cast
  on one's own character. Either could become the gamemaster's.
